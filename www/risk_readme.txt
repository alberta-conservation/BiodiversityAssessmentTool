This file describes the data included with the Risk Assessment Report Download
from the Biodiversity Assessment Tool developed by the 
Alberta Biodiversity Conservation Program. 



- decline_plot.png
  Plot showing estimated probabilities of population loss relative to a baseline 
  scenario of historic climate and no oil sands development, but including forest
  harvesting, across a range of potential outcomes from 1% loss to 20% loss  

- decline-probabilities.csv
  Data used to create the decline_plot.png 
  - pct_decline: the percentage of populaition loss relative to a baseline scneario 
  - baseline_OS: probability of outcome under historic climate with oil sands development
  - climate: probability of outcome under climate change with no oil sands development
  - climate_OS: probability of outcome under climate change with oil sands development

- density_plot.png
  - Plot of probability densities of potential population sizes at year 50 under  
    4 alternative scenarios 

- population-scenario.csv  
  Data used to create the density_plot.png
  - inter: the iteration number
  - scenario: the scenario name
  - pop_size: the estimated future population outcome
